### About us
**Kicker:** About us
**Heading:** Run by families, for families.

Since 1986, Betty Spears has remained a not-for-profit, parent-governed centre where decisions are shaped by families and community, not a corporate model.

Our volunteer Board of Directors — all parents themselves — meets monthly, and every family is welcome to attend. From time to time we ask for a hand with fundraising or a working bee, always optional, because many hands make light work.

**Feature grid:**

- **Familiar faces** — Educators who stay for years, not months — so children grow up surrounded by people they know and trust.

### Our story (founder)
**Kicker:** Our story
**Heading:** A long history and a genuine sense of home.

The centre is named after the late Betty Spears (1926–2012), who - alongside June deLorenzo - worked tirelessly to secure state and federal funding in the 1980s for shift-working families, especially mothers, at the State Transit Authority. What they built was, to our knowledge, one of the first 24-hour child care centres in Sydney. We’re no longer open around the clock, but it’s with pride that we carry that same spirit forward today.

### Part of the neighbourhood
**Kicker:** Part of the neighbourhood
**Heading:** Woven into the local community.

Many of our families have shared several children with us over the years, and some of our parents and educators came to Betty’s as children themselves. Each year our preschoolers visit Tempe Public School to explore the library and kindergarten classrooms ahead of starting “big school”, the local fire brigade drops by to show off the truck and talk fire safety, and the year winds up with a shared lunch at a nearby Thai restaurant — a tradition the children look forward to.

### Our educators
**Kicker:** Our educators
**Heading:** Your child gets to know the people caring for them.

The long-serving nature of our educators is one of Betty’s greatest strengths. Families know they can trust familiar faces, stable routines and deep relationships that grow over time.

Every room has a university-qualified Early Childhood Teacher alongside Diploma-qualified educators, with more staff per child than the regulations require — plus our own trusted list of permanent relief staff, so a sick day never means an unfamiliar face.

### Our philosophy — C.H.I.L.D.R.E.N.
**Kicker:** Our philosophy
**Heading:** C.H.I.L.D.R.E.N.

Written with families and educators, and revisited every year, this is what guides how we care for your child and each other.

- **C — Community & belonging** — A community-based centre fostering respect and understanding for ourselves, each other and the wider community.
- **H — Health & wellbeing** — Emotional and physical wellbeing come first, so every child feels secure enough to fully engage and enjoy their day.
- **I — Inclusion** — We welcome all children and families and work to maintain a culturally safe, culturally inclusive centre where everyone feels connected.
- **L — Learning from interest** — Children are capable, competent co-contributors in their own learning, exploring at their own pace and following their interests.
- **D — Development: being & becoming** — We meet children where they are and help them grow, while supporting educators’ ongoing development too.
- **R — Relationships & rights** — Meaningful relationships built on trust and respect, so Betty’s feels like a home away from home for children and families alike.
- **E — Environment** — Time, space and freedom for children to investigate and explore the world in their own way.
- **N — Nature** — Daily opportunities to explore our large natural outdoor space, and to learn what it means to care for the world around us.

### Daily routine
**Kicker:** Daily routine
**Heading:** Play-based, and led by the children themselves.

At Betty’s, learning grows naturally from children’s interests, relationships and everyday play. We believe children thrive when they have time, space and trusted adults who understand their needs.

Play is how children make sense of the world — building curiosity, confidence and the courage to try new things.

Our educators follow each child’s lead, so every day feels like exploring rather than being taught at.

#### Curriculum
**Kicker:** Curriculum
**Heading:** Guided by the Early Years Learning Framework.

Our play-based program follows Australia’s national curriculum for early childhood, which sees childhood as a time of belonging, being and becoming. It’s built around five learning outcomes: a strong sense of identity, connection to and contribution to their world, a strong sense of wellbeing, confident and involved learning, and effective communication.

Each room keeps a daily diary linking back to the curriculum, and every child has their own learning journal — a record of their development, interests and experiences that you’re welcome to read, and take home to add to, any time.

#### Life at Betty’s
**Kicker:** Life at Betty’s
**Heading:** Nature, food and shared routines — every day.

Betty’s is known for its generous outdoor spaces, where children run, dig, climb, notice and explore. Fresh air, open-ended play and time in nature are part of the rhythm of the day — alongside fresh food and calm, connected mealtimes. Even our youngest toddlers have their own patch to explore, with a sensory walk and vegetable garden in the 0–2s outdoor area — picking herbs and watering the garden are big favourites.

#### Food & nutrition
**Kicker:** Food & nutrition
**Heading:** Fresh food, shared routines and healthy habits.

Our cook makes real food from scratch — low in salt and sugar, with fresh fruit and veg delivered every week. An early childhood nutritionist checks every menu, and we run the “Munch and Move” program alongside it, so healthy habits are just part of the day.

We serve breakfast, morning tea, lunch, afternoon tea and a late snack, with a bowl of fresh fruit always on hand in every room.

For toddlers and preschoolers, meals happen in a dedicated dining room where an educator sits with a small group to share food and conversation — more like a family table than a canteen. Foods are served in separate bowls with tasting saucers on offer, so children can self-select at their own pace without feeling pressured by foods they’d rather avoid.

We work with families to cater to dietary requirements, so every child feels comfortable and included at the table.

### FAQs
**Kicker:** FAQs
**Heading:** Questions families often ask.

Can’t find what you’re after? Just give us a call or send an email — we’re happy to help.

**What ages do you accept?**
Betty Spears welcomes children from approximately 6 weeks to 6 years, with the centre structured around care, play and early learning across different age groups.

**What are your opening hours?**
The centre is open Monday to Friday, 7:30am–6:00pm, with a strong focus on long-day care and smooth family routines.

**Are you open all year round?**
We’re open 50 weeks a year and close for two weeks over Christmas and New Year, when fees aren’t payable.

**How does Child Care Subsidy work?**
Families may be eligible for Child Care Subsidy through Services Australia. Our team can explain the process and guide you through the application and enrolment steps.

**What is your National Quality Standard rating?**
Betty Spears was assessed against the National Quality Standard in August 2023 and rated as Meeting the Standard overall, while Exceeding in three of the seven Quality Areas.

**How does the waiting list work?**
Families can submit a waiting list application and pay the applicable waiting list fee. Once your application is received, we’ll confirm it and contact you when a place becomes available.

**How long can the waiting list take?**
Waiting times vary depending on age group and availability. We recommend joining the list as early as possible and staying in touch with the centre as your circumstances change.

**Do you provide meals?**
Yes. Fresh meals are prepared at the centre and planned with children’s nutrition, routines and dietary needs in mind.

**Do you provide nappies?**
No, daily fees include everything your child will need in a day except formula for babies and nappies for all children using them.

**How do we arrange a visit?**
You can contact the centre to arrange a visit, speak with the team and see the environment in person. We’re always happy to show families around and answer questions.
